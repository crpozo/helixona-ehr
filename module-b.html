Amendment / Addendum

A signed encounter cannot be edited. To add or correct information, create a dated addendum that becomes part of the legal record.

Why use an addendum (not edit): The original note is the legal record at the moment of sign. Amending preserves the original verbatim and appends new content with a fresh timestamp, signature, and reason — meeting HIPAA, payer audit, and 21CFR Part 11 standards.
